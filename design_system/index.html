Visual identity · internal review draft · 08 Sep 2026

Five ways GRECSOC could look.

A working proposal for the leadership group: five complete identity systems for our publications on LinkedIn, X and Instagram. Each is built for the same mission and the same intellectual base — they differ in tone, in who they speak to first, and in the job they do best. Read the brief, walk the five, then use the last two sections to choose.

Hub for social sciences & AI risk mitigation Programmes: BlueDot AGI Strategy · public talk on AI safety Intellectual base: Flavia Costa, Vidas artificiales & the Observatorio

01 — The brief every direction answers

One mission, one voice, many surfaces

GRECSOC is a social-sciences hub working to mitigate the risks of artificial intelligence. Concretely, that means two things: proposing legal regulation, and producing studies that give regulators, universities and the press something solid to cite. Our public face has to carry both the rigour of a research group and the clarity of an advocacy campaign.

Our activities right now are the BlueDot Impact AGI Strategy course, which we host, and an open talk on AI safety and what the social sciences can do about it. Both need graphics that work as a square on Instagram, a wide card on LinkedIn, and a post image on X — from one system, without redrawing.

The intellectual reference point is Flavia Costa — researcher at CONICET, director of the Observatorio de Tecnologías, Sociedad y Ecosistemas Digitales (UBA) — her group, and her book Vidas artificiales: del accidente pandémico a las guerras de la IA (Taurus, 2024). Her vocabulary — the tecnósfera, the accident, AI as a form of life rather than an event, the erosion of the intermediate bodies that hold society together — runs through all five directions below.

  • Audience order

    Legislators and their staff, university committees, journalists who cite sources, and the students in our courses — roughly in that priority.

  • Register

    Serious, non-sensationalist, but willing to name a risk plainly. Never doom-marketing; never techno-optimism.

  • Bilingual by default

    Spanish first, English second. Type and layout must hold a headline in both without breaking.

  • Made by a small team

    Whatever we pick, two or three people must be able to produce a week of posts in an afternoon.

Headlines, quotes and event details in the mock-ups are placeholder copy for design evaluation only. Lines drawn from Vidas artificiales are paraphrased and flagged as “a partir de”; nothing here is a verified quotation and no wording is final. Judge the systems, not the sentences.

Direction A

Tecnósfera — the institution

A Tecnósfera Reads as a research institute Best for: policy briefs, citations, press

The policy-brief register

This system reads as an institution before it reads as a campaign. It borrows the authority of the printed policy paper: a transitional serif (Spectral), a footnoted layout, ink navy on the colour of good paper, and a single oxidised-brass accent. It is the visual register of Costa's Observatorio and of Vidas artificiales as a Taurus imprint — the look a legislative aide or a journalist trusts on sight. Use it for the studies, the regulatory proposals, and anything with a bibliography.

Direction B

Ecosistemas — the environment

B Ecosistemas AI as environment, not event Best for: essays, the studies programme

Formas de vida, no titulares

This is the register of Costa's Observatorio de Tecnologías, Sociedad y Ecosistemas Digitales: artificial intelligence understood as an environment we now live inside, a form of life rather than a single event. Piazzolla — drawn by the Argentine foundry Huerta Tipográfica — carries the headlines in warm clay and forest green, over a hand-drawn network motif. Less alarm, more attention. It suits the essay series, the long threads, and the studies programme where the argument is ecological, not episodic.

Direction C

Protocolo — the system

C Protocolo For the people who build the systems Best for: high-volume publishing, technical credibility

We can read the model card and the annex

A strict 12-column grid, IBM Plex Mono carrying every label, coordinate annotations in the margin, one flat cobalt accent. This system signals that GRECSOC's social scientists can read a model card and a regulatory annex with equal fluency — that we speak to the labs and the ministries in their own register. It is also the most production-friendly of the five: a rigid template a small team can fill quickly and consistently, week after week.

Direction D

Umbral — the imprint

D Umbral Publishes like a small press Best for: essays, reviews, the reading group

Cada posteo es una página

GRECSOC produces text before it produces graphics — essays, reviews, interviews, the reading group around Vidas artificiales. Umbral treats every post as a page from that imprint: Newsreader's literary italic, a ruled title-page frame, oxblood and slate on uncoated cream, folios instead of hashtags. It is unhurried and it trusts the reader. Use it for the monthly column, author conversations, and anything with a byline.

Direction E

Señal — the campaign

E Señal Made to be shared, not only read Best for: open letters, calls to regulate

Pide algo y dice dónde firmar

GRECSOC's other job is to move policy — open letters, coalition statements, calls for a specific law. Señal is built for that: Archivo at speaking weight, one confident persimmon, statements set large enough to read as a thumbnail. It asks for something and tells you where to sign. Quick to produce and easy to co-brand — a coalition of ten organisations can all post the same asset. Loud in message, not in ornament; this is the alternative to alarmism, not a version of it.

02 — Side by side

How the five compare

None of these is only one thing — each can flex. What the table captures is the default each system reaches for, and the trade-off that comes with it.

  A · Tecnósfera B · Ecosistemas C · Protocolo D · Umbral E · Señal
Speaks to first Legislators, academics, press Readers, students, essayists Labs, ministries, technical staff The book's audience; long-form readers The public; allied organisations
Tone Measured, authoritative Reflective, warm Precise, systematic Literary, unhurried Direct, mobilising
Type feel Printed policy paper Literary magazine Spec sheet / interface Title page / small press Campaign poster, clean
Best format LinkedIn, long PDFs Essays, X threads, carousels Diagrams, carousels, LinkedIn Essays, reviews, newsletters Instagram, X, Stories
Main risk Reads as dry / slow Undersells urgency Reads as cold / startup-y Too slow for news or alerts Tips into slogan
Production load Medium Medium–High Low Medium Low
Fit with Costa The Taurus / Observatorio register The ecosistemas digitales frame The tecnósfera as infrastructure Vidas artificiales as an object Her call to regulate, made public

A common recommendation: pick one as the house style and name one or two others as modes. A natural split is A · Tecnósfera as the house style for studies and institutional posts, D · Umbral for the essay and reading-group strand, and E · Señal reserved for regulation campaigns. B and C are strong single-identity choices if the group would rather not run more than one.

03 — Your turn

How to weigh in

Three questions to answer, ideally before we meet. Bring a first choice and a second choice, not a consensus — we will resolve it together.

1 · Which direction?

Rank A / B / C / D / E. Say what your first choice gets right and what worries you about it.

2 · One identity or several?

Do we want a single system, or a house style plus one or two named modes? If several, which combination?

3 · The name

“GRECSOC” is a working acronym. Options on the table include Grupo de Reflexión y Estudios en Ciencias Sociales and Grupo de Riesgos Emergentes y Ciencias Sociales. Bring a preference or a new one.
