Visual identity · internal review draft · 08 Sep 2026

Four ways GRECSOC could look.

A working proposal for the leadership group: four complete identity systems for our publications on LinkedIn, X and Instagram. Each is built for the same mission and the same intellectual base — they differ in tone, in who they speak to first, and in how much alarm they carry. Read the brief, walk the four, then use the last two sections to choose.

Hub for social sciences & AI risk mitigation Programmes: BlueDot AGI Strategy · public talk on AI safety Intellectual base: Flavia Costa, Vidas artificiales & the Observatorio

01 — The brief every direction answers

One mission, one voice, many surfaces

GRECSOC is a social-sciences hub working to mitigate the risks of artificial intelligence. Concretely, that means two things: proposing legal regulation, and producing studies that give regulators, universities and the press something solid to cite. Our public face has to carry both the rigour of a research group and the clarity of an advocacy campaign.

Our activities right now are the BlueDot Impact AGI Strategy course, which we host, and an open talk on AI safety and what the social sciences can do about it. Both need graphics that work as a square on Instagram, a wide card on LinkedIn, and a post image on X — from one system, without redrawing.

The intellectual reference point is Flavia Costa — researcher at CONICET, director of the Observatorio de Tecnologías, Sociedad y Ecosistemas Digitales (UBA) — her group, and her book Vidas artificiales: del accidente pandémico a las guerras de la IA (Taurus, 2024). Her vocabulary — the tecnósfera, the accident, AI as a form of life rather than an event, the erosion of the intermediate bodies that hold society together — runs through all four directions below.

  • Audience order

    Legislators and their staff, university committees, journalists who cite sources, and the students in our courses — roughly in that priority.

  • Register

    Serious, non-sensationalist, but willing to name a risk plainly. Never doom-marketing; never techno-optimism.

  • Bilingual by default

    Spanish first, English second. Type and layout must hold a headline in both without breaking.

  • Made by a small team

    Whatever we pick, two or three people must be able to produce a week of posts in an afternoon.

Headlines, quotes and event details in the mock-ups are placeholder copy for design evaluation only. Lines drawn from Vidas artificiales are paraphrased and flagged as “a partir de”; nothing here is a verified quotation and no wording is final. Judge the systems, not the sentences.

Direction A

Tecnósfera — the institution

A Tecnósfera Reads as a research institute Best for: policy briefs, citations, press

The policy-brief register

This system reads as an institution before it reads as a campaign. It borrows the authority of the printed policy paper: a transitional serif (Spectral), a footnoted layout, ink navy on the colour of good paper, and a single oxidised-brass accent. It is the visual register of Costa's Observatorio and of Vidas artificiales as a Taurus imprint — the look that a legislative aide or a journalist trusts on sight. Use it for the studies, the regulatory proposals, and anything with a bibliography.

Direction B

El Accidente — the alarm

B El Accidente Built to stop the scroll Best for: launches, rapid response

Every technology invents its accident

The name is Paul Virilio's thesis, and it sits at the centre of Costa's argument: to invent the ship is to invent the shipwreck. This system is engineered to interrupt a feed — hazard amber and alarm red, Archivo at poster weight, copy datelined like a bulletin. It is the register for announcing the AI-safety talk, for a course-enrolment deadline, and for rapid-response posts the day an AI story breaks. Deliberately loud; use it as the exception, not the default.

Direction C

Ecosistemas — the environment

C Ecosistemas AI as environment, not event Best for: essays, the studies programme

Formas de vida, no titulares

This is the register of Costa's Observatorio de Tecnologías, Sociedad y Ecosistemas Digitales: artificial intelligence understood as an environment we now live inside, a form of life rather than a single event. Piazzolla — drawn by the Argentine foundry Huerta Tipográfica — carries the headlines in warm clay and forest green, over a hand-drawn network motif. Less alarm, more attention. It suits the essay series, the long threads, and the studies programme where the argument is ecological, not episodic.

Direction D

Protocolo — the system

D Protocolo For the people who build the systems Best for: high-volume publishing, technical credibility

We can read the model card and the annex

A strict 12-column grid, IBM Plex Mono carrying every label, coordinate annotations in the margin, one flat cobalt accent. This system signals that GRECSOC's social scientists can read a model card and a regulatory annex with equal fluency — that we speak to the labs and the ministries in their own register. It is also the most production-friendly of the four: a rigid template a small team can fill quickly and consistently, week after week.

02 — Side by side

How the four compare

None of these is only one thing — each can flex. What the table captures is the default each system reaches for, and the trade-off that comes with it.

  A · Tecnósfera B · El Accidente C · Ecosistemas D · Protocolo
Speaks to first Legislators, academics, press The general feed; broad public Readers, students, essayists Labs, ministries, technical staff
Tone Measured, authoritative Urgent, interruptive Reflective, warm Precise, systematic
Type feel Printed policy paper Bulletin / poster Literary magazine Spec sheet / interface
Best format LinkedIn, long PDFs Instagram, X, stories Essays, X threads, carousels Diagrams, carousels, LinkedIn
Main risk Reads as dry / slow Reads as doom-marketing Undersells urgency Reads as cold / startup-y
Production load Medium Low Medium–High Low
Fit with Costa The Taurus / Observatorio register The accident thesis, literally The ecosistemas digitales frame The tecnósfera as infrastructure

A common recommendation: pick one as the primary identity and borrow a second as a named “campaign mode”. The most natural pairing is A as the house style for reports and institutional posts, with B reserved for launches and rapid response. C or D can play the same second-style role if the group prefers their register.

03 — Your turn

How to weigh in

Three questions to answer, ideally before we meet. Bring a first choice and a second choice, not a consensus — we will resolve it together.

1 · Which direction?

Rank A / B / C / D. Say what your first choice gets right and what worries you about it.

2 · One identity or two?

Do we want a single system, or a house style plus a campaign mode? If two, which pair?

3 · The name

“GRECSOC” is a working acronym. Options on the table include Grupo de Reflexión y Estudios en Ciencias Sociales and Grupo de Riesgos Emergentes y Ciencias Sociales. Bring a preference or a new one.
