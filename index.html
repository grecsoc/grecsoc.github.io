Deciding how GRECSOC looks.

Two working drafts for the leadership group to review before we meet. Open each, read the brief, and bring a shortlist — not a consensus.

GRECSOC is a social-sciences hub for mitigating the risks of AI — proposing legal regulation and producing studies. Current programmes: the BlueDot Impact AGI Strategy course and an open talk on AI safety and the social sciences. Intellectual base: Flavia Costa, her group, and Vidas artificiales (Taurus, 2024).
