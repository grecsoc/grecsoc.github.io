Twenty marks, drawn as sketches.

Twenty logo concepts for GRECSOC, each rendered in one colour so it is judged as form — the palette and typeface come from the design-system page. Every mark is grounded in the group's vocabulary: the observatory, the threshold (umbral), AI as an environment, the accident, the intermediate bodies that hold society together, the call to regulate. 01 — The twenty

Concepts to shortlist

These are concept sketches, not finished artwork — proportions, weights and the wordmark lock-up would all be redrawn for whichever direction wins. The point is to agree on an idea first: an instrument that watches, a threshold, a living system, an accident, a fabric coming apart, a demand for a rule.

02 — Your turn

How to weigh in

1 · Shortlist three

Pick your top three by number. One line each on why.

2 · Which idea?

Name the concept that should carry the group — observatory, threshold, ecosystem, signal, accident, regulation — even if none of these exact marks is right yet.

3 · Pair it up

Say whether a mark should be tied to a specific design direction (e.g. 05 Malla with B · Ecosistemas, 10 Señal with E).

Leave your notes
