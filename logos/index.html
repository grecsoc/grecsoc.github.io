Visual identity · logo marks · internal review draft

Twenty marks, drawn as sketches.

Twenty logo concepts for GRECSOC, each rendered in one colour so it is judged as form — the palette and typeface come from the design-system page. Every mark is grounded in Costa's vocabulary — the accident, AI as an environment, the umbral, the intermediate bodies that hold society together — read through the visual language of the AI-safety field: its institutes, evaluators and funds tend toward light-cones, apertures, alignment vectors, compasses, bounding frames and governors. Shortlist three.

Each card shows the mark plus, below the rule: · app-icon size · reversed on ink · locked up with the wordmark

01 — The twenty

Concepts to shortlist

These are concept sketches, not finished artwork — proportions, weights and the wordmark lock-up would all be redrawn for whichever direction wins. The point is to agree on an idea first: a light-cone, an aperture that watches, a fabric coming apart, a signal with a spike, a frame around the technology, a compass off true north.

02 — Your turn

How to weigh in

1 · Shortlist three

Pick your top three by number. One line each on why.

2 · Which idea?

Name the concept that should carry the group — observatory, threshold, ecosystem, signal, accident, regulation — even if none of these exact marks is right yet.

3 · Pair it up

Say whether a mark should be tied to a specific design direction (e.g. 05 Malla with B · Ecosistemas, 10 Señal with E).

Leave your notes

Open an issue on the repo, or reply on the thread where this link was shared. Reference marks by number.
